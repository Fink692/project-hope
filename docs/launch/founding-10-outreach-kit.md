# Founding 10 outreach kit

This kit is for permission-based outreach to legitimate charities and nonprofit networks. Its purpose is to earn ten unique, email-verified applications without harvesting personal information, buying lists, or presenting interest as revenue.

Until a public managed deployment and transactional email are verified, use the private LinkedIn-message call to action in the [release post](linkedin-release-post.md). Do not send people to an application form that cannot complete email confirmation.

## Early-partner profile

Prioritize a charity that:

- has a named staff or volunteer champion who can join onboarding and feedback sessions;
- currently coordinates contacts, volunteers, programs, grants, communications, or impact reporting across disconnected tools;
- is willing to begin with synthetic or low-sensitivity data while security and operating procedures are validated;
- accepts that AI output is optional, bounded, and reviewed by a person; and
- can explain one concrete workflow it wants Project Hope to improve during the pilot.

Do not position the first pilot as clinical software, emergency response, autonomous case management, or a replacement for safeguarding judgment. High-sensitivity beneficiary data stays out until the deployment's built-in MFA/recovery, privacy, backup, restore, retention, and incident-response gates are complete.

## Seven-day close-the-loop message

Hi [name]—I’ll close the loop after this note so I don’t add noise to your inbox. If simplifying [their stated workflow] becomes a priority later, I would be glad to reconnect. The public release remains here: https://github.com/Fink692/project-hope/releases/tag/v1.8.0

## Fifteen-minute fit conversation

1. **Two minutes:** what Project Hope is, what the Founding 10 is, and what it is not.
2. **Five minutes:** the charity's current workflow, users, biggest friction, and sensitivity of the data involved.
3. **Four minutes:** invitation, installation, role controls, and one relevant end-to-end workflow.
4. **Two minutes:** hosting, support, price, data responsibilities, cancellation, and the synthetic-data-first boundary.
5. **Two minutes:** decide together—apply, schedule a deeper session, revisit later, or decline.

Ask these qualification questions:

- What work is currently split across spreadsheets, inboxes, or separate apps?
- Who would own the pilot internally, and who else needs to approve it?
- What is one measurable improvement worth testing in the first 30 days?
- What personal or sensitive information touches that workflow?
- Would the organization start with synthetic or low-sensitivity data?
- Which accessibility, language, device, or training needs must the pilot support?
- What would make the organization stop the pilot?

## Evidence and follow-through

- Keep outreach notes in an access-controlled system, never in the public repository.
- Record only a work contact and the minimum context needed to follow up.
- Enter an applicant into the Founding 10 metric only through the consented application and email-confirmation flow.
- Count each normalized verified email once. A demo, fixture, repeat submission, purchased contact, or verbal expression of interest is not a verified application.
- Report `verified`, `qualified`, `pilot`, and `converted` separately.
- Review every outbound message before sending it. Do not automate cold sends or imply endorsement by a network.
- Honour a decline immediately and stop follow-up after the close-the-loop message.

The evidence command and definitions are in the [commercial readiness runbook](../commercial-readiness.md). The verified count remains **0/10** until real production confirmation evidence says otherwise.
