# LinkedIn release post — Project Hope 1.6

## Post

After a long stretch of building, testing, rebuilding, and polishing the details users should never have to think about, I’m proud to release Project Hope 1.6. 🌱

This is the release where Project Hope becomes the product I set out to make: install the app, sign in, and get to work.

Charities do essential work, but too many are still asked to stitch that work together with disconnected spreadsheets, inboxes, documents, and tools that assume an IT team is standing by.

Project Hope brings contacts, volunteers, schedules, documents, grants, communications, impact work, accessibility tools, and carefully bounded AI into one connected workspace.

Version 1.6 makes the first day feel like a real product:

- ✅ Secure first-owner setup, team invitations, role controls, resending, and revocation
- ✅ Private, expiring account links for new and existing teammates
- ✅ Privacy-preserving account recovery and hardened browser/native sign-in
- ✅ Windows, Apple silicon macOS, and Linux installers, plus connected web and mobile clients
- ✅ One managed source of truth—no stray local record copies to reconcile
- ✅ Optional, bounded AI that remains subject to human review

The part I’m proudest of is not a single feature. It is the care underneath them: a charity should not need to understand containers, databases, model runtimes, or deployment architecture just to use good software.

This release passed 48 backend tests, 11 web journeys with automated accessibility checks, 7 AI-gateway tests, a live local-model smoke suite, production builds across every client, dependency audits, and production configuration checks.

I’m now opening the Founding 10: ten charities willing to test Project Hope honestly and help shape what comes next. The managed Founding Partner pilot is CAD $149/month after the workspace is live. There is no application fee, no card collected to apply, and no payment obligation until scope and responsibilities are agreed in writing.

If you work with a charity—or know someone who does—I would be grateful if you shared this.

To ask about a Founding 10 place, send me a private LinkedIn message with “FOUNDING 10” and your charity’s name. Please do not post personal, client, donor, volunteer, or service-user information in the comments.

After working on this for a long time, it feels deeply meaningful to finally put Project Hope into the world. It is ready for its first real charity partners—and I’m incredibly proud of the care that went into getting it here.
